        Two ways to pay
        
    Per-request escrow, and prepaid sessions
    
        
    An API provider lists an endpoint with a price. A client pays for one request in an ERC-20 (here a
          Portaldot native asset, reached through the pallet-assets ERC-20 precompile). The payment waits in
          escrow in a Solidity contract on Portaldot's EVM. The provider settles it once it has served the request,
          or refunds it. An ink! registry on Portaldot's WASM VM keeps the provider's public record.
          Everything on this page is read live from the testnet by your browser.

    Payment token: a native asset seen as an ERC-20
    
      
    Two test assets, one per payment path. The prepaid sessions and the hosted seller (endpoints 3 and 4) are
        priced in pUSD, asset 7777, Portaldot's own test dollar. The per-request escrow run (endpoints 1 and 2) used
        tUSDm, asset 43907 "Portal Meter Test USD", which we created for it; that is the asset read below.
    
      
    Portaldot exposes every pallet-assets token to Solidity at
        0x{asset id}…00010000. The supply below is read twice: once through the EVM (totalSupply()
        on the precompile) and once from Substrate storage.
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Endpoints and provider record
    
      
    Price and payee come from MeterSettlement (EVM). The URL and the served / refunded /
        confirmed / disputed counts come from MeterRegistry (ink!), keyed by the provider's EVM address. On a session
        endpoint, one "served" is one anchored batch of many requests.
